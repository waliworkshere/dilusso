About Dilusso

Fifteen years of kitchen hardware

Dilusso began by supplying hinges and drawer runners to carpenters. Over more than 15 years it has grown into a manufacturer of complete kitchen systems, present in 8 countries.

Our range covers hinges, plastic cabinet joints, drawer systems, pull-out baskets, sinks, hoods, doors, wardrobes and finished kitchens, for homeowners, joinery workshops and contractors.

Dilusso is in Oman, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Turkey, Italy, Spain, China and Malaysia.

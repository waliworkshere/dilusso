About Dilusso

Twenty years of kitchen hardware in Oman

Dilusso began by supplying hinges and drawer runners to carpenters in Muscat. Over more than 20 years it has grown into a manufacturer and retailer of complete kitchen systems, with eight showrooms in the capital.

Our products are designed with our team in Italy and made in our own production in Turkey and Egypt. Everything is shipped to Oman and sold here, so customers get factory-made quality with local advice, stock and after-sales support.

Today Dilusso supplies fittings, sinks, hoods, doors, wardrobes and finished kitchens to homeowners, joinery workshops and contractors across the Sultanate.
